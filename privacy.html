Privacy Policy — KÖLGƏ HQ

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Who we are

KÖLGƏ HQ is a private business assistant operated by KÖLGƏ Group, Baku, Azerbaijan. It has one user: its owner. Contact: kolge.inc@gmail.com.

2. What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent (Google OAuth), the app accesses the Gmail account of its owner (kolge.inc@gmail.com): message contents and metadata, and drafts. It does not access any other person's Google account.

3. How the data is used

Only to provide the app's own functions for the owner: sorting incoming messages and preparing reply drafts. The app never sends an email without the owner's explicit approval. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or sold or shared with third parties.

4. Processing by AI service providers

To classify messages and prepare drafts, the text of selected messages may be sent to AI service providers (such as OpenAI or Anthropic) through their APIs. It is used only to produce the requested result for the owner.

5. Storage and retention

Data is stored on a private server operated by the owner. Access credentials are kept in restricted files on that server. Data is kept until the owner deletes it.

6. Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can request deletion of stored data by writing to the contact address above.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

KÖLGƏ HQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
